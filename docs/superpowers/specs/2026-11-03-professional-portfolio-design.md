# Professional portfolio refresh

User request: modernise spsarolkar.github.io for professional readers while
keeping/updating its existing content. On 3 November 2026 the user chose
"Use your judgment and proceed with the recommended redesign" and delegated
the visual and implementation details. No further design checkpoint is needed.

## Intent and approach

Lead with current AI and solutions architecture work, relevant experience and
selected public projects. Retain the technical depth in writing, projects, CV,
credentials and the wider archive. Link the separate personal lab at
sunilsarolkar.in. Keep Jekyll, the locked dependencies, GitHub Pages, permalinks,
post filenames, six numbered project routes, comments/search and existing media.

The chosen approach is a professional portfolio in the existing Jekyll system.
A styling-only refresh would leave the stale role and cluttered hierarchy; a
framework migration adds maintenance without improving this content-led site.

## Visual direction (implementation choices)

Use a white/soft neutral canvas, dark slate text, restrained blue-green accents,
strong sans-serif headings, fine rules, generous whitespace and a consistent
reading width. Preserve light/dark controls with a complete dark palette. Use
existing photographs and project artwork where useful; no decorative animation,
invented metrics, gradient hero, skill percentages or stock illustrations.

Homepage: current role and concise introduction; selected work; areas of
expertise; recent writing; concise credentials; contact. Full career details,
all education, employer training badges and existing learning records remain
on the CV. Secondary archive links stay available in the footer. Keep primary
navigation small: Profile, Projects, Writing, CV, with search/theme utilities
and a clear personal-lab link. Mobile navigation must remain keyboard usable.

## Content rules

Use the latest user-provided AI Solutions Architect PDF and the already refreshed
personal-lab CV data for current career facts. Update present employment to the
June 2026 Principal Solution Architect role and end DB employment in May 2026.
Preserve original authored posts and project bodies, original media, existing
recognition, language/interests and older downloadable PDF URLs. Add the new
résumé PDF path, supplied fifth employer badge and two verified credential
records. Preserve all 20 existing Credly records, without inventing dates for
skill badges. Fix known broken navigation/image references without changing
article dates or existing inbound URLs.

Do not reinterpret historical experimental results as current product guarantees.
Distinguish personal research from professional outcomes. Existing PDF-supported
CV claims may be retained in the CV; homepage summaries do not amplify disputed
experimental accuracy. No private employer information beyond supplied public
material, new application dependencies or account/access changes.

## Verification and delivery

Build the untouched commit as a baseline and compare generated routes with the
new production output. Check existing article text/media preservation, all
public routes and referenced local assets, anchors, résumé hash, credential
records and real desktop/mobile light/dark layouts. Review a code-heavy article
and long CV; check search and menus after production CSS processing. Use a
separate codex branch and bounded commits. Keep logs and tooling outside source.
Publish through the existing GitHub Pages workflow once validated; if access
requires user action, leave a concrete tested branch/preview and report it.


Verification required two narrow existing-build corrections: real Sass-input
cache fingerprints and matching image/CSS prerequisites across the two existing
Pages workflows. Their audiences, permissions and publishing targets stay the
same. Existing article text and routes remain intact.
